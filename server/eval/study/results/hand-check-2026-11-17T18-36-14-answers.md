4. **Restart the computer and try again**: Save your open work, restart the computer, and then click the PDF attachment in your email again. A restart often clears up file association problems.

> I noticed the OneDrive icon down by the clock has a little pause sign on it and I tried closing and reopening the folder window which did not change anything and my files still are not syncing up to the cloud even though I was working on them all afternoon so I am not sure what is going on and would appreciate someone taking a look when possible.

**Hidden cause:** OneDrive syncing was paused (battery saver paused it).  
**What fixes it:** Click the OneDrive icon and choose Resume syncing.

1. **Check that your internet connection is working**: Open a website in your browser to confirm you are online. OneDrive cannot upload files while the connection is down.
2. **Resume syncing in OneDrive**: Click the OneDrive cloud icon near the clock and look for a message saying syncing is paused. If it is, choose Resume syncing and wait a minute to see if your files start uploading.
3. **Quit and reopen OneDrive**: Right-click the OneDrive icon near the clock and choose Quit OneDrive. Then open OneDrive from the Start menu to restart it and check whether the files begin syncing.
4. **Check for Windows updates**: Open Settings, go to Windows Update, and select Check for updates. Install any available updates, then check whether OneDrive syncs again.
5. **Restart the computer**: Save your work, restart the computer, and then check the OneDrive icon near the clock to see whether the files are syncing.
