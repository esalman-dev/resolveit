# Hand check (101 answers)

For each one: would an ordinary employee who follows these steps fix the hidden cause? Write **yes** or **no** after "Your answer:". Also say if any step is unsafe.

## t002 · Unable to open PDF file
> Good morning, when I click on a PDF attachment in my email, a window pops up asking me to choose how to open the file. I am not sure which program I should pick, and I would appreciate some help getting the files to open properly. Thank you for your time.

**Hidden cause:** No working default app is set for PDF files.  
**What fixes it:** Choose Open with and pick Edge (or Acrobat Reader), and set it as the default app for .pdf files.

1. **Pick a PDF reader from the list**: In the window asking how to open the file, choose Microsoft Edge or Adobe Acrobat/Reader if you see either one. Tick the box that says to always use that app for PDF files so the file opens the same way next time.
2. **Open the attachment in your web email**: Open your email in a web browser such as Edge or Chrome instead of the email app. Click the PDF attachment there, and it should display in the browser.
3. **Close and reopen your email app**: Close your email program completely, open it again, and then click the PDF attachment once more.
4. **Check for updates**: Open Windows Update from the Start menu and select Check for updates. Install any updates it finds, then try the PDF again.
5. **Restart the computer**: Save your open work, restart the computer, and then try opening the PDF attachment again.

**Hidden cause:** Outlook's Focused Inbox sorts the manager's emails into the Other tab.  
**What fixes it:** Check the Other tab and move the emails to Focused (Always move to Focused), or turn off Focused Inbox.

1. **Check your Junk and Spam folders**: Open the Junk or Spam folder in your email app. Look for emails from your manager that may have been filtered there. If you find them, open one and mark it as not junk.
2. **Check for a storage warning**: Look for a message or banner in your email app that says your mailbox storage is full. Note the warning so you can tell the technician if you see one.
3. **Ask your manager to send a test email**: Ask your manager to send a short test email with the subject line 'Test' and tell you when it was sent. This shows whether new messages are reaching you now.
4. **Restart your email app**: Close your email app completely and open it again. Wait a minute so it can check for new messages.
5. **Turn Wi-Fi off and on**: Click the Wi-Fi icon and turn Wi-Fi off, wait about 10 seconds, then turn it back on. Check that your email app can reconnect and load new messages.

## t017 · Laptop cannot find any Wi-Fi

2. **Check the laptop's Wi-Fi switch**: Look for a physical Wi-Fi switch on the side of the laptop, or a function key with a wireless antenna icon. Make sure it is turned on.
3. **Check whether other devices see the office network**: Pick up a phone or another computer near you and open its Wi-Fi list. If the office network appears there, the problem is likely with this laptop, not the network.
4. **Turn Wi-Fi off and on**: Open the Wi-Fi quick setting from the bottom-right corner, switch Wi-Fi off, wait a few seconds, then switch it back on. Wait about 10 seconds for networks to appear.
5. **Restart the laptop**: Save any open work, then restart the laptop from the Start menu. After it starts up, open the Wi-Fi list again to see whether networks appear.

## t019 · Excel not responding
> Good morning, Excel stopped responding this afternoon while I was working in a sheet I use for monthly reports. The window is greyed out with Not Responding at the top and I cannot save or close it normally.

**Hidden cause:** Excel is stuck recalculating a very large workbook and stopped responding.  
**What fixes it:** Wait a minute or two; if it stays frozen, end Excel in Task Manager and reopen it, then restore the AutoRecover copy.

1. **Wait a few minutes for Excel to recover**: Large monthly report sheets can take a while to respond. Give Excel a few minutes before you do anything else.
2. **Close Excel from Task Manager**: Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager, select Microsoft Excel in the list, and choose End task. This closes only the frozen Excel window.
3. **Reopen Excel and check for recovered files**: Open Excel again and look in the Document Recovery panel on the left for a version of your report. Choose the most recent version and save it under a new name.
4. **Restart the computer if Excel still freezes**: Restart Windows from the Start menu, then open the report again. This clears any background problem that is keeping Excel stuck.

3. **Set the monitor to its recommended resolution**: In the same Display settings, open the Resolution list for the new monitor. Choose the option marked Recommended, which matches the monitor's native size.
4. **Restart the laptop**: Restart the laptop so the new display settings are applied to both screens. Save your work first.
